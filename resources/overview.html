Expressed means the format renders a document with the feature differently from the same document without it - if the two outputs are identical, the feature is gone. Unchanged is the stricter test: convert out, read back, compare the pandoc AST node for node.

† carve / carve-ast are not pandoc formats - they go through the pandoc-carve bridge (source lane and AST lane). native is pandoc's own AST printer, shown as the 66/66 reference.

Measured against the released pandoc 3.10.2. On the 2026-08-17 nightly the djot writer gained raw-content passthrough, which moves djot from 52 to 56 expressed and 33 to 35 unchanged; the four probes are raw inline and raw block for html and latex. No other format changes between the two builds.

Formats trimmed to one row per family (aliases like html5, epub3, jats_publishing score the same as their parent). Full matrix of all 76 pandoc writers (34 of them also readers), per-probe grids and the metadata results: docs/report.pdf
